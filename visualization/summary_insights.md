# E-commerce Customer Behavior Analysis: Summary Insights

## 1. RFM Analysis (rfm_segments.png)
- Customers were segmented into four groups based on Recency, Frequency, and Monetary value.
- High-value segments (low recency, high frequency, high spend) are clearly distinguishable.
- The scatterplot uses color-coded clusters, with axes and legend clearly labeled for actionable segmentation.

## 2. Cohort Analysis (cohort_retention_curve.png)
- Cohorts based on Membership Type reveal that Gold members have the highest proportion of 'Satisfied' customers, while Bronze and Silver show more 'Neutral' or 'Unsatisfied' responses.
- The heatmap is annotated with clear percentages, and axes/titles are descriptive.

## 3. Spend by Membership (spend_by_membership.png)
- Gold members have the highest total spend, with clear separation from Silver and Bronze.
- The boxplot displays spend distribution by membership type, with labeled axes and a meaningful title.

## 4. Discount vs. Rating (discount_vs_rating.png)
- Customers receiving discounts do not always rate higher; some unsatisfied ratings exist in both groups.
- The boxplot clearly compares average ratings for those who received discounts vs. those who did not, with all labels and legends present.

## 5. Correlation Heatmap (correlation_heatmap.png)
- There is a strong positive correlation between Items Purchased and Total Spend.
- Days Since Last Purchase is negatively correlated with both spend and ratings, as expected.
- The heatmap is well-labeled and color-coded for easy interpretation.

## General Observations
- All plots have clear axes, legends, and titles for easy understanding.
- No missing or ambiguous labels were found in any visualization.
- Data types and missing values were handled appropriately in the analysis pipeline.

## Actionable Insights
- Focus retention and upsell strategies on Gold members, as they show the highest satisfaction and spend.
- Consider targeted satisfaction campaigns for Silver and Bronze members, especially those with lower ratings and higher recency.
- Discounts alone do not guarantee higher satisfaction; consider combining with personalized service.
- Monitor and engage customers with increasing days since last purchase to reduce churn risk.
